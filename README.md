PROMPT -

Create a modern, professional, and elegant landing page for "Lóvitta Laboratório de Análises Clínicas", a clinical analysis laboratory based in Brazil.

---

## BRAND IDENTITY

- **Name:** Lóvitta Laboratório de Análises Clínicas

- **Logo:** The logo features a circle with a stylized "L" and a heartbeat/graph line inside it, in dark navy/black. Below is the name "Lóvitta" in bold serif font and "Laboratório de Análises Clínicas" in a smaller, elegant sans-serif subtitle.

- **Color Palette:**

  - Primary: Deep navy/dark charcoal (#1a1a2e or #2c2c3e)

  - Accent: Crimson/deep red (#c0392b or #b71c1c) — used in the heartbeat line in the logo

  - Background: Clean white (#ffffff) and soft light gray (#f7f7f7)

  - Text: Dark gray (#333333)

- **Tone:** Trustworthy, modern, clinical precision, human warmth. Think premium healthcare — not cold or sterile, but professional and welcoming.

- **Typography:** Elegant serif for headings (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond), clean sans-serif for body (Inter or Lato).

---

## PAGE SECTIONS (in order)

### 1. NAVBAR

- Logo on the left

- Navigation links: Início, Exames, Resultados, Sobre Nós, Contato

- CTA button (top right): "Agendar Exame" — styled in deep red/crimson with white text

- Sticky navbar that changes opacity/background on scroll

### 2. HERO SECTION

- Full-width, visually impactful

- Background: A subtle gradient overlay (dark navy to transparent) over a clean, high-quality image of a modern laboratory or a professional healthcare professional

- Headline (large, serif font): "Precisão que cuida. Resultados em que você confia."

- Sub-headline: "Exames laboratoriais com tecnologia de ponta e atendimento humanizado."

- Two CTA buttons: "Agendar Exame" (primary, red) and "Ver Exames" (secondary, outlined)

- Small trust badges below the CTAs: icons for "Resultados Online", "Coleta em Domicílio", "Laudos Digitais"

### 3. ABOUT / DIFERENCIAIS SECTION

- Section title: "Por que escolher a Lóvitta?"

- 3–4 cards with icons, each highlighting a key differentiator:

  - 🔬 Tecnologia de Ponta

  - 🏠 Coleta Domiciliar

  - 📱 Resultados Online 24h

  - 👨‍⚕️ Equipe Especializada

- Clean card layout, soft shadow, minimal design

### 4. EXAMES / SERVICES SECTION

- Section title: "Nossos Exames"

- Grid of exam categories with icons/illustrations:

  - Hemograma e Bioquímica

  - Hormônios

  - Microbiologia

  - Imunologia

  - Genética Molecular

  - Check-up Completo

- Each card: icon + name + short description + "Saiba mais" link

- Subtle hover animation on cards

### 5. HOW IT WORKS (Passo a Passo)

- Section title: "Como funciona?"

- 3-step horizontal flow:

  1. 📅 Agende seu exame online ou por telefone

  2. 🧪 Realize a coleta no laboratório ou em casa

  3. 📲 Acesse seus resultados com segurança online

- Clean icons, numbered steps, connected with a subtle line/arrow

### 6. RESULTS / ONLINE ACCESS CTA

- Full-width banner section

- Background: dark navy or gradient

- Text: "Acesse seus resultados de forma rápida e segura."

- Large input field for "CPF ou código do pedido" + button "Acessar Resultado"

- Small supporting text: "Disponível 24 horas por dia, 7 dias por semana"

### 7. TESTIMONIALS

- Section title: "O que nossos pacientes dizem"

- 3 testimonial cards with:

  - Star rating (5 stars)

  - Quote text

  - Patient name and exam type

- Soft card style, subtle background

### 8. CONVENIOS / INSURANCE PARTNERS

- Section title: "Convênios Aceitos"

- Horizontal logo carousel/grid of insurance plan logos (use placeholder logos)

- Clean, minimal layout

### 9. CONTACT / LOCATION SECTION

- Section title: "Fale Conosco"

- Two-column layout:

  - Left: Contact info (address, phone, email, hours), WhatsApp button (green)

  - Right: Embedded Google Maps placeholder

- Instagram link and social icons

### 10. FOOTER

- Logo (white version)

- Navigation links

- "Política de Privacidade" and copyright: "© 2025 Lóvitta Laboratório. Todos os direitos reservados."

- Social icons: Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp

---

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Built with React + Tailwind CSS

- Fully responsive (mobile-first)

- Smooth scroll and subtle entrance animations (fade-in on scroll)

- WhatsApp floating button (bottom right corner) — green with WhatsApp icon

- "Agendar Exame" sticky CTA visible on mobile as a bottom bar

- Use placeholder images from Unsplash (medical/lab themed)

- All text in Brazilian Portuguese

---

## DESIGN INSPIRATION

- Clean, premium medical aesthetic similar to: Mendelics, IACS, LAC Pompeia

- Not overly colorful — restrained palette, lots of white space

- Subtle geometric patterns or lab-themed background textures in some sections

- High contrast between sections to guide the eye
